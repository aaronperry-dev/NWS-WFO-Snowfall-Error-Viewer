How to read this

  • Red = the forecast was too high on average; blue = too low. Each cell is the mean of (forecast − observed) storm-total snowfall over the season's storms, in inches per storm.
  • Forecast: the official WFO-edited NDFD snowfall grid, issued about 12 h before each storm. Observed: NOHRSC National Snowfall Analysis. Storms: CWA-mean total of 1.5 in or more.
  • Single-season averages rest on few storms (5–24 per office); intervals are wide. See each office's storm count and 90 % interval.
  • Hatched cells are excluded radar beam-blockage artifacts in the observation analysis.
